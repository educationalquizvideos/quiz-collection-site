Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Overview

Quiz Collection is an application used to manage and analyze video content published to YouTube.

Information We Access

The application uses the YouTube API to access information associated with the authorized YouTube account. This may include video information, playlists, channel information, and YouTube Analytics data.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the YouTube API is used solely to manage, publish, and analyze Quiz Collection video content.

Information Sharing

The application does not sell or share YouTube account information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and API information are stored securely and are used only to provide the application's functionality.

Data Deletion

You may revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time through your Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the application owner using the support email associated with the application.
